Tips

Built around what actually works for both of you.

Beyond patties on the BBQ - keep them cold

Form them straight from the fridge, press a small indent in the middle so they don't puff up. Medium-high heat, 3-4 min per side. Mozzarella on last minute with lid closed to melt.

Gold buns - toast them on the BBQ

Cut side down on the grill for 30-60 seconds while the patties rest. Adds crunch and stops the bun going soggy from the burger.

Mozzarella block vs pre-shredded

Slicing fresh from the block melts way better on burgers and paninis. Pre-shredded has a coating that stops it melting as cleanly.

Scotch bap rolls - for Sammies only

Toast the cut side on the panini press for 30-60 seconds before adding fillings. Stops it going soggy. Gold buns are for burgers only.

Tomato cream pasta - sauce tips

Saute garlic in butter first, add canned tomatoes and simmer 5 min, then stir in heavy cream off the heat. Toss with pasta and parmesan. Penne or rigatoni holds the sauce best.
